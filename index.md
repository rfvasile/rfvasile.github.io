## About me

Hi, my name is Florian! I completed my Master's degree at the University of Bologna
specialising in machine learning. My thesis explored neurosymbolic approaches to
solve linear algebra problems, which involved pairing neural networks with symbolic
libraries for structuring the reasoning process around external tools for more
accurate computations.

Before that, I completed a joint honours bachelor's degree in Computer Science
and Mathematics at the University of Manchester, focusing on automated reasoning.
My bachelor's thesis was a CDCL SAT solver for propositional satisfiability.
The [Projects](#Projects) section below provides references to some of my previous
work including reports and code.

I've also spent four and a half years working as a software engineer: a year at
Diamond Light Source on accelerator controls, then three and a half years
writing software for embedded devices in warehouse and agricultural automation.

You can find my **[resume here](static/cv.pdf)**.


## Prior Experience

I worked as a full-stack/Python software engineer for 3.5 years at [Onit
Group](https://onit.it/en) and [Agronica](https://agronica.info/). The work
involved writing software for embedded devices used in warehouse equipment
control, most of the time operated through GUI interfaces, but micro-services
for data collection played also a significant role.

Prior to this, I spent one year working as a software engineer at [Diamond
Light Source](https://www.diamond.ac.uk/), working on a Python library for
accelerator controls. This interface replaced the existing MATLAB-based
workflows to ease experimentation, enabling precise magnet control and
alignment.
